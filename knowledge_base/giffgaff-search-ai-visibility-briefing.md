## 1. Executive Summary

As search behaviour shifts toward AI-driven conversational engines, brand visibility is no longer just about ranking giffgaff.com. It is about ensuring giffgaff is recommended by the third-party authorities that AI engines cite. 

Our analysis of recent AI search data reveals three core strategic realities for giffgaff:

1. **High visibility in core strengths, weak in expansion areas:** giffgaff is highly recommended by AI for "Flexible / SIM-only" and "PAYG" queries (78% and 68% brand mention rates respectively), but struggles significantly in "Handsets / Phones" (22%) and "Bundles" (14%).
2. **Comparison sites are the new gatekeepers:** AI engines overwhelmingly rely on aggregator and comparison sites (uSwitch, MoneySuperMarket, MoneySavingExpert, Which?) to form their recommendations. An earned-first strategy must prioritise PR and partnership efforts with these tier-one authorities.
3. **Perplexity is the hardest to influence:** While ChatGPT and Copilot frequently recommend giffgaff (59% and 53% of the time), Perplexity is far more conservative (28%), relying heavily on deep research and consumer advocacy sites.

## 4. Platform Nuances

Not all AI engines evaluate brands the same way. Splendid's strategy must account for the differing 'personalities' of the major platforms:

*   **ChatGPT (59.2% visibility):** The most favourable to giffgaff. It relies heavily on commercial comparison sites and is very likely to recommend giffgaff for SIM-only and PAYG queries.
*   **Copilot (53.0% visibility):** Similar to ChatGPT, but relies slightly more on official operator sites and Microsoft's Bing index.
*   **Google AI Overviews (49.6% visibility):** Highly responsive to giffgaff's B Corp messaging (citing it 87.9% of the time for relevant queries), but relies heavily on competitors for handset and bundle queries.
*   **Perplexity (27.8% visibility):** The hardest engine to penetrate. Perplexity favours deep research, regulatory bodies (Ofcom), and strict consumer advocacy (Which?). To win here, giffgaff needs mentions in long-form, authoritative journalism and rigorous independent testing reports (e.g., Opensignal, RootMetrics).

## 5. Strategic Recommendations for Splendid

Based on this illustrative snapshot, we recommend the following focus areas for Splendid's earned-first strategy for giffgaff:

1.  **Dominate the Aggregators:** Shift PR focus toward securing top placements, reviews, and "best of" list inclusions on uSwitch, MoneySuperMarket, and Which?. These are the primary data sources feeding AI recommendations.
2.  **Close the Handset Gap:** giffgaff is seen by AI almost exclusively as a SIM-only provider. To win visibility for handset searches, Splendid must generate earned media coverage specifically reviewing giffgaff's refurbished phone offering and flexible handset financing in tier-one tech and consumer press.
3.  **Activate B Corp Status Beyond Google:** While Google recognises giffgaff's B Corp status, ChatGPT and Copilot do not associate the brand with ethical/sustainable queries. PR campaigns should focus on linking giffgaff's B Corp credentials with its commercial offerings in mainstream media to train these LLMs.
4.  **Leverage Community (Reddit):** Reddit is the 12th most cited source overall. A community-led strategy that encourages genuine, positive discussion about giffgaff on UK personal finance and mobile subreddits will directly impact AI recommendations.
