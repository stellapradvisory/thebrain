Stella Bayles Launches Strategic Advisory Practice After 12
Years at CoverageBook

Industry authority says the PR industry must evolve how it plans, measures
and communicates earned, as AI search makes the stakes higher than ever

London, July 2025. Stella Bayles, one of the PR industry’s most recognised voices on

measurement, search and digital communications, has launched Stella PR Advisory, an

independent strategic consultancy helping PR agencies and in-house communications teams

raise their game in AI search, earned performance and modern measurement.

The move follows 12 years at CoverageBook, the PR reporting tool used by thousands of in-

house teams and agencies worldwide. Prior to that, Bayles built one of the world’s first digital

PR/SEO teams at Propellernet, driving 85% agency revenue growth, and served as Director at
AnswerThePublic, introducing search insight to the PR industry ahead of its acquisition by NP
Digital.

The era of earned performance

“Something fundamental is changing in how earned communications is expected to work,” says
Bayles. “Internally at brands, silos are breaking down. PR, search, social and performance

teams that used to operate separately are being asked to demonstrate how they contribute to
the same business outcomes. The expectation of what earned should deliver, and how it should

be reported, has shifted significantly. And most of the industry has not caught up yet.”

This is not, she is clear, simply about adding AI search to an agency’s service offering. It is about

how the entire discipline of earned communications is understood, planned, measured and
communicated. “It is about how we talk about earned from a strategic level, how we feed

genuine results insight back into strategy, how we demonstrate business outcomes rather than

activity, and how we report in a way that is continuous, transparent and performance-related
rather than retrospective and campaign-led. PR has been far too slow to make that shift.”

AI search has accelerated the urgency. Fifteen years ago, search teams were waiting for PR to
step forward. They needed earned coverage with genuine recommendation, and only a handful
of agencies recognised that, adapted their language and claimed significant budgets as a result.
Most did not move. “The situation now is broader,” says Bayles. “AI search visibility is not a

separate discipline. It is an outcome of good PR. AI systems are trained on earned reputation
and recommendation: the coverage, the citations, the trusted voices that say a brand is worth
knowing about. That is entirely the work of PR. But the industry will only be able to claim it if it
starts communicating, reporting and measuring in ways that are more aligned with the rest of

digital marketing: data-led, evidence-based and transparent about performance. AI search has

pushed us to make that shift. The agencies that make it will grow their influence and their

budgets significantly.”

What genuinely needs to change

“What passes as strong reporting for a Head of PR does not meet the bar for a senior Head of

Digital or a business lead seeking commercial outcomes,” says Bayles. “Search and

performance budget holders are not impressed by coverage volumes and inflated reach figures.

They want to understand what earned activity is doing for the business, and they want to see it

regularly. To genuinely communicate with those budget holders, earned teams need to be more

data-driven and evidence-led in their strategy and how they report. That requires someone who

has operated on both sides of that conversation.”

Having worked directly with hundreds of agencies and in-house teams, she has seen the same

gaps emerge consistently. “I have watched the industry miss opportunities before. This time, I

want to help the people who are ready to make the change.”

She also raises a concern about the wave of new AI search and GEO experts emerging across
the industry. “Understanding how LLMs work is only part of it. The harder part is understanding
the objectives and language of search and digital performance budget holders who have been in
this space long before AI arrived, people who think in terms of business outcomes, attribution
and commercial impact. Knowing about AI platforms does not give you that. And if you cannot
speak their language and feed it back into strategy and measurement, you will not be credible in

those conversations.”

Stella PR Advisory works with agency founders, leadership teams and senior in-house
communications directors on strategic advisory engagements. Bayles is also completing a book
on modern PR measurement and will be launching a new podcast soon.
