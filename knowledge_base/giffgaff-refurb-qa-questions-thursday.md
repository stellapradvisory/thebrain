# giffgaff Refurb — Questions for Thursday 11am Q&A
*Prepared by Stella Bayles, Stella PR Advisory — August 2026*
*For use by Jess Proudfoot / Splendid Communications in client Q&A session*

---

## Commercial & Audience

**1.** Do they want this refurbished phones activity to be present and visible during the peak trading periods (Q4 and Black Friday) so they're part of the consideration stage of the customer journey? i.e. negative misconceptions about quality and reliability turned around by Black Friday? This will lead to strategy, tactic decisions and messaging. *(This question relates to no.18 too)*

**2.** Can we have access to benchmark metrics such as sales of refurb phones in this key period last year and in 2024?

**3.** Has there ever been a peak in refurb phone sales? If so, when and why?

**4.** Do you have attribution or assisted-conversion data showing which channels influence refurbished purchases?

**5.** Is there customer research or audience data to back the claim that the 18–35 'switched-on crowd' have a misconception about refurb phones? Research detail, stats, tools or surveys used would be useful for insight, strategy and benchmarking for impact measurement.

**6.** Do you have existing target customer insight or audience targeting insight for the 18–35 'switched-on crowd'? Demographics (gender, location, income), psychographics (values, interests, lifestyle choices) and behavioural data (search habits, preferred social media platforms, purchasing triggers) will be the foundation of a prompt behaviour map. Anything from existing sales data, analytics, CRM tools, customer surveys or internal persona work would be great. If there isn't existing data, could we interview the customer relations team?

**7.** Do you have existing customer data on where the 18–35 switched-on crowd's journey typically starts i.e. referral data on where they currently arrive from?

**8.** The brief mentions the audience spends 35–45 hours a week on their phones. Do you have any data on how they search when they're in the research phase for a phone purchase — whether that's Google, TikTok search, Reddit, or AI assistants? Even directional signals from your analytics or paid search team would be useful.

**9.** The brief references screen quality and battery health as the top barriers. Do your customer success team have any insight on specific phrases or questions coming through community forums or social listening that reflect these concerns? The verbatim language people use is very useful for insight and strategy.

**10.** How can we use the giffgaff community itself? Are there opportunities to surface authentic member stories, FAQs or expert answers as part of this campaign?

**11.** Do you have evidence we can use beyond product specifications? Return rates, battery longevity, customer satisfaction, Trustpilot data, warranty claims, repeat purchase rates, repair rates or anything else that objectively proves refurbished quality?

---

## Content & Source Authority

**12.** Which third-party sites are currently driving the most referral traffic to your refurb pages — comparison sites, review sites, tech press? And do you know which of those are also ranking for refurb queries in organic search? This tells us which publishers are already authoritative in this space and should be priority earned media targets.

**13.** Are you currently tracking AI referral traffic in your analytics — sessions arriving from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai? If so, which pages on giffgaff.com are receiving that traffic right now?

**14.** Does giffgaff have any existing editorial or long-form content about refurbished phones — guides, comparison pages, FAQs? Do you know how they're currently performing in organic search? Understanding what's already there helps us avoid duplicating effort and focus on what needs to be created or strengthened.

**15.** The brief cites the Dove/Reddit example as an inspiration. Are you open to Reddit as a channel — both as a source of authentic audience language and as a platform where earned content could live?

---

## Measurement & Timeline

**16.** Do you have any existing subscriptions to AI visibility or GEO monitoring tools — Profound, Otterly, Semrush AI, or similar? If so, we'd love access so we can work directly within your existing stack rather than duplicating it. And if not, it's worth knowing preferences so we can factor tool costs into the approach.

**17.** The brief references the Responsible Marketing Manifesto as a lens for all activity. Are there any specific third-party publishers, creator categories, or platforms that are off-limits or need sign-off? We want to make sure our earned media targets are fully aligned.

**18.** LLM visibility typically takes 4–8 weeks to build after content is published and picked up by authoritative sources. Is there appetite to start seeding content and earning coverage now — ahead of the campaign — so that by Black Friday the AI search layer is already working? Or does everything need to align with the 1 November launch?
