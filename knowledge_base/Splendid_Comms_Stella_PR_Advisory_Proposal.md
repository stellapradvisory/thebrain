P H A S E   O N E

Under the Bonnet Review

A detailed look at how the agency currently works, understanding where Splendid is today and what needs to

change to get where you want to be.

Covering: planning and strategy, measurement and reporting, team capability, technology and data, client

opportunities, skills gaps and hiring.

Output: Clear priorities across people, process, technology and proposition.

P H A S E   T W O

Building the Performance Framework

Developing the measurement and insight framework that moves PR from retrospective reporting to continuous

strategic insight.

TRADITIONAL PR ASKS:

THE NEW FRAMEWORK ANSWERS:

What happened?

What's happening right now?

How much coverage did we get?

Why is it happening?

Where did we land?

What should we do more of, or stop?

A consistent classification layer beneath flexible campaign objectives creates comparable data across clients,

teams and time, unlocking benchmarking, pattern recognition and AI-ready insight.

GEO Measurement: AI Visibility & Reputation

The framework will include how Splendid measures brand presence in AI-generated answers. As more

decisions begin with ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, the question shifts from whether a brand

ranks to whether AI systems represent it accurately and consistently. Key metrics include AI visibility rate,

citation share, share of voice and message accuracy, tracked monthly against a consistent prompt set. This is

PR evaluation, not SEO — and exactly where Splendid should lead.

P H A S E   T H R E E

Embedding the Change

Adapting planning processes, evolving reporting, implementing technology, training teams and developing client

conversations around performance.

The aim isn't to create analysts. It's to help every consultant feel confident using data as part of their strategic

thinking.
