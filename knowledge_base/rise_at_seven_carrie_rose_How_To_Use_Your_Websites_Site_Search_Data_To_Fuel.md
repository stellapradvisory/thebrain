The Best Travel Marketing Campaigns That Changed How We Discover Destinations
Share
Need inspiration? Sounds like we all do after how travel marketing has changed.
Don't worry, here at
Rise At Seven
. We've been working with the biggest travel brands in the world, the ones that want to build creative campaigns and we love inspiration.
The campaigns that capture attention today aren’t simply beautiful TV adverts or glossy destination videos. The best travel brands are creating moments people actively search for, share with friends, save for later and rediscover across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Google.
Discovery has become fragmented.
People don’t just search
“best places to visit in Italy”
on Google anymore. They search TikTok for hidden gems, YouTube for itineraries, Instagram for inspiration and Reddit for honest recommendations before they ever reach a booking site.
That means the campaigns making the biggest impact are no longer just creative they’re engineered to travel across platforms.
In this roundup we’ve collected some of our favourite international travel campaigns from airlines, hotels, tourism boards and travel brands that successfully combined creativity with discoverability.
1: Duolingo x Carnival Cruise Line –
The 5-Year Cruise
The internet's favourite green owl teamed up with Carnival to give away a five-year cruise. Rather than feeling like a travel promotion, the campaign leaned into Duolingo's chaotic social personality.
🛳️ A fictional
5-year cruise
visiting
195 countries
🌍 Travel across
all 7 continents
🗣️ Learn
40+ languages
with Duo onboard
🎥 Hero campaign film, fake booking site and social content
📱 Huge organic engagement across Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok and Reddit as people debated
This is a brilliant example of two brands fully committing to a ridiculous creative idea. Rather than simply announcing a partnership, Duolingo and Carnival created a campaign that people wanted to investigate, share and discuss. It blurred the line between reality and satire, played perfectly into Duo's online persona and generated conversation far beyond the travel industry. It proves that the most memorable travel marketing doesn't always have to sell a destination; it just has to create a moment people can't ignore.

2: Virgin Voyages x TikTok –
Boatchella
Probably the boldest creator activation of 2026.
Virgin Voyages partnered with TikTok to hand over an entire cruise ship to
1,103 creators
, transforming a three-night sailing to the Bahamas into a floating content festival. Rather than inviting a handful of influencers, Virgin built an experience designed to generate thousands of authentic perspectives across food, travel, wellness, entertainment and lifestyle.
The ship itself became the campaign, with creator workshops, immersive brand activations, live performances (including GRYFFIN at Virgin's Beach Club in Bimini), and appearances from Sir Richard Branson. Every element was designed to be filmed, shared and discovered on social media.
