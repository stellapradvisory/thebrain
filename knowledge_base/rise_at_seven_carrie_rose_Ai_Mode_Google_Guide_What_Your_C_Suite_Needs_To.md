🚨 Google just announced what it calls the biggest upgrade to the Search box in more than 25 years 👇
A year ago Google launched AI Mode - and I (Carrie) said "AI mode is no longer just a switch users can turn on or off, but a tab which users can use as their default - with hints that it will take over the whole SERP". Since then it has surpassed one billion monthly users, with queries more than doubling every quarter since launch and yesterday... it really did take over the whole SERP.
Yesterday (19th May 2026) Google IO happened - the biggest upgrade to the search box in more than 25 years. And within about 20 minutes of the keynote, our inbox started filling up with the same question from senior marketers: should we be worried?
Honestly? It depends on how you’ve been playing the game.
Please note:
this is going to impact every type of marketer - brand marketers, social media marketers, organic marketers and paid! And in this post I am going to share in very simple terms what this means, what C suites should know. Learn how our
AI SEO agency services
help brands improve visibility across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and other generative search platforms.
This isn't just a Google update, or another algorithm update. It’s a message to marketers about how consumers will now discover your brand at each stage of the journey. It turns search from a public directory into a
deeply personalised assistant
- and if you’re not ready for that shift, your brand could slowly and literally disappear from the search journey without anyone noticing, not even you!
Stop being so hard to find.
If your exec team hasn’t had
the conversation
yet about what this means, here it is.
TL;DR: Search is no longer about keywords or even just content. It’s about people.
AI Mode doesn’t just give answers - it knows
you
as a person. It’s pulling data from Gmail, Calendar, Maps, YouTube, your past searches, your shopping receipts, and probably that one weird Google Doc from 2017. It’s building a personalised profile of you and serving content that fits
your
preferences,
your
habits, and
your
intent.
So what does that actually look like?
Hate onions? Google won’t show you recipes with onions.
Got a Paris trip coming up? You’ll get travel tips near to the hotel you've booked, not general search results.
Always running at 10pm? You’ll see gyms and routines that match your schedule.
Been talking about breakups in your email? It might suggest new hobbies or therapy.
This is no longer a one-size-fits-all search engine. This is
your
search engine.
The way consumers find you - has drastically switched to 1-1 hyper personalised results and Googles latest update in May 2026 is moving customers more and more to conversational search - killing off single keywords.
This isn’t a technical breakdown for your SEO team - there are plenty of those. This is for the CMO, the CEO, the Commercial Director who needs to understand in really basic and commercial terms what actually changed, why it matters, and what to do about it.
