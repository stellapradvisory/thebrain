Starting today, SparkToro audience research reports now contain brand affinity data, showing the companies, products, and brand names an audience is most familiar with and/or purchases from.
When we first received requests for this data, my cofounder Casey went to work finding a way to use our existing audience data sources, and, genius that he is, uncovered a solution. Granted, it took a few weeks of tweaking and tuning to get the data into shape, but I’ve been consistently impressed with it in my extensive pre-launch testing. Now, I’m looking forward to y’all having a go.
The new Brand Affinity section can be found in the “Keywords and Prompts” dropdown, as shown above. And, of course, I’ve got a quick video walkthrough on how to use this data (spoiler: customers asked us for it primarily to do either A) for agencies/consultants to show off their knowledge of a problem space in a pitch process and B) for in-house marketers to study the brands their customers knew best for content, marketing, and competitive intel research)
