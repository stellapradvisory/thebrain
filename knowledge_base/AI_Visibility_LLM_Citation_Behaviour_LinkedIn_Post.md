# AI Visibility — LLM Citation Behaviour Research (ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini)

*Source: LinkedIn post (screenshot saved July 2026)*
*Topic: AI visibility, LLM citation behaviour, cross-engine citation overlap, Ebb tool, PR and AI influence*
*Related post: [AI_Visibility_LLM_Fan_Out_Research_LinkedIn_Post.md](./AI_Visibility_LLM_Fan_Out_Research_LinkedIn_Post.md)*

---

Well, it turns out that 'AI not being one thing' is a hill that I'm very much willing to die on and will probably go on about for quite some time, until its proven otherwise...

Building on the fan-out research I shared earlier this week (different LLMs running wildly different numbers of fan-out query searches), I've been doing some more digging — this time into how ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini handle **citations** and if there's any difference in their approaches.

Side note: having Ebb at my disposal is SUCH a treat; it's proving brilliant for on-going visibility tracking for our clients (as you'd hope — that's what we built it for!) but the ability it's given me to find truly actionable insights to help our clients show up in ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini is even more valuable.

So, the latest findings... I analysed **36,128 citations** (i.e. what domains actually get pulled into answers), taken from more than **650 responses** from ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, for prompts we're tracking across retail, travel, fintech and B2B. And again, the behaviour per LLM is completely different.

Most of the time, they simply don't cite the same content. Taking any domain cited by at least one of the three, across the full dataset; **only 4.1% were cited by all three.**

I think this is really important to acknowledge, because I keep hearing that PR is what you need to do to influence AI. And while that might be true, it's not specific enough to act on. We shouldn't be trying to influencing 'AI', rather Claude, or ChatGPT, or Gemini. And to do that, we need to understand how they behave and for PR, their approach to citation specifically.

---

## Specific Findings from the Research

**B2B prompts (choosing a digital marketing agency):**
- **ChatGPT** mostly cited agencies' own sites, third-party directories and listicles *(described as "a horrible, spammy tactic")*
- **Claude and Gemini** mostly ignored directories, more commonly citing agencies' own sites — more than 70% of the time

**Consumer-facing category:**
- **ChatGPT** stuck almost entirely to brands' own sites
- **Claude** surfaced obscure niche blogs
- **Gemini** leant hard on Reddit and YouTube

---

## Key Data: Cross-Engine Citation Overlap

*Analysis of 36,128 citations from more than 650 prompts spanning retail, travel, fintech and B2B*

**To what extent do Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini cite the same domains in responses?**

| | ChatGPT | Claude | Gemini |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT** | — | 10.1% | 7.9% |
| **Claude** | 10.1% | — | 22.0% |
| **Gemini** | 7.9% | 22.0% | — |

> **Only 4.1%** of domains analysed were cited by all 3 LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini).

*Note: Claude and Gemini share the most citation overlap (22%), while ChatGPT has the least overlap with both others.*

---

## Key Takeaways

- **LLMs cite completely different domains** — only 4.1% overlap across all three
- **"Influencing AI" is not specific enough** — you need to target Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini individually
- **ChatGPT (B2B):** Cites directories and listicles heavily — but this is considered a low-quality signal
- **Claude & Gemini (B2B):** Prefer agencies' own sites (70%+ of citations)
- **Consumer categories:** Each LLM has a completely different source preference (brands vs niche blogs vs Reddit/YouTube)
- **Ebb tool** is being used for ongoing client visibility tracking and generating actionable insights per LLM
- This research is based on a large but not exhaustive set of prompts, captured at one point in time — more data runs needed for confidence
