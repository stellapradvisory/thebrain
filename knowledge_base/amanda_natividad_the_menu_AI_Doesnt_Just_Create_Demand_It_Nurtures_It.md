Although I suppose if you don’t have time for any of this, then these are the important parts:
Similarweb was trying to answer the question: when AI introduces or recommends a brand, does that recommendation appear to change what people do afterward?
Yes.
Users who received an AI recommendation were 2 and a half times more likely to visit that brand’s website within the next 7 days.
And when they did visit that website, how did they show up? Through branded search.
