## Standing Strategic Priorities

### 1. Growing an engaged 20–30s audience — CRITICAL

> **This is a confirmed, standing business priority for giffgaff and must inform all search behaviour research and AI search strategy work.**

giffgaff is actively investing in building a loyal, engaged audience among 20–30-year-olds. This is evidenced across multiple signals:

- The **Studio B "The Board" proposal** (July 2026) is built entirely around this audience — described as "aspirational compromisers" who are recalculating their route through work, side hustles, and financial independence. Studio B explicitly frames this as a **massive generational shift** and argues giffgaff has the community infrastructure to own it.
- The **Fair-play Summer campaign** (live, July 2026) and the **Broadband Boss Academy** (May 2026) are both content-led programmes targeting this age group with practical, utility-driven messaging.
- The **"better Monzo / Google" ambition** (per Jess Proudfoot's briefing note) signals giffgaff wants to be a trusted utility brand for this generation — not just a SIM provider.
- giffgaff's **four editorial content pillars** (Beat the Game, Smart Moves, Back Yourself, Game Changers) are all oriented around the practical and financial concerns of young adults navigating an unfair system.

### Implications for Splendid's AI Search Strategy

How 20–30-year-olds search is fundamentally different from older demographics, and this matters enormously for GEO and AI search strategy:

- This cohort is **disproportionately likely to use AI-native search** (ChatGPT, Perplexity, TikTok search, Reddit Answers) rather than traditional Google.
- Their queries are **conversational, problem-framed, and comparison-oriented** — e.g. "what's the best SIM for someone who doesn't use much data some months?" rather than "cheap SIM only deals".
- They are **heavy Reddit users** — Reddit is already the 12th most-cited source in giffgaff's current AI citation data, and Hellen Benavides has explicitly flagged Reddit as a goldmine for human-led campaign thinking.
- They are **sceptical of brand-led content** and trust peer recommendations, community voices, and third-party comparison sites — the same sources that AI engines cite.
- They are **mobile-first** and likely to encounter AI search through app-based interfaces (ChatGPT app, Perplexity app, Google app AI Overviews).

This means that winning AI search visibility for giffgaff among 20–30s requires a different content and earned media strategy than winning it among older demographics. Specifically:

1. **Community-sourced content** (Reddit, giffgaff community forum) needs to be part of the earned strategy, not just press coverage.
2. **Problem-framed content** (written from the user's question, not the brand's answer) will perform better in AI citation.
3. **Peer-voice formats** (real member stories, creator collaborations, community Q&As) will carry more weight with LLMs than brand-authored content.
4. **TikTok and YouTube** are increasingly search engines for this age group — content optimised for these platforms feeds AI search as well as social discovery.
