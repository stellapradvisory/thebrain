**AGREEMENT**

**1. Definitions**

1.1 In this Agreement, except to the extent expressly provided otherwise:

"Agreement" means this agreement including any Schedules, and any amendments to this Agreement from time to time;

"Charges" means the charges and other payable amounts specified in Schedule 1 (Services particulars) and elsewhere in this Agreement;

"Client Materials" means all works and materials supplied by or on behalf of the Client to the Consultant for incorporation into the Deliverables or for some other use in connection with the Services;

"Deliverables" means the strategic advisory outputs, measurement frameworks, research, and such other deliverables as the parties may agree in writing from time to time;

"Effective Date" means the date of execution of this Agreement;

"Intellectual Property Rights" means all intellectual property rights wherever in the world, whether registrable or unregistrable, registered or unregistered, including copyright, database rights, trade secrets, know-how, trade marks, and rights in designs;

"Services" means the consultancy services specified in Schedule 1 (Services particulars).

---

**2. Term**

2.1 This Agreement shall come into force upon the Effective Date.

2.2 This Agreement shall continue in force until terminated in accordance with Clause 9 or any other provision of this Agreement.

---

**3. Services**

3.1 The Consultant shall provide the Services to the Client in accordance with this Agreement.

3.2 The Consultant shall provide the Services with reasonable skill and care.

---

**4. Deliverables & Intellectual Property**

4.1 The Consultant shall deliver the Deliverables to the Client in accordance with the timetable set out in Schedule 1.

4.2 The Consultant hereby grants to the Client a non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual and irrevocable licence to copy, store, distribute, publish, adapt, edit and otherwise use the Deliverables (excluding any pre-existing methodologies or frameworks owned by the Consultant) for the Client's business purposes.

4.3 The Consultant retains all Intellectual Property Rights in her pre-existing methodologies, frameworks (including the Modern Earned Media Measurement Framework and all associated tools and approaches), and proprietary materials. These are licensed to the Client for use in connection with the Services but are not transferred or assigned.

---

**5. Confidentiality**

5.1 The Consultant agrees to keep confidential all non-public information concerning the Client and any end-clients of the Client disclosed during the course of the Services, and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Client.

5.2 Where a separate Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) has been signed between the parties, that NDA shall take precedence in the event of any conflict regarding confidentiality obligations.

5.3 The confidentiality obligations in this Clause shall survive termination of this Agreement indefinitely.

---

**6. Charges & Payments**

6.1 The Client shall pay the Charges to the Consultant in accordance with Schedule 1.

6.2 All amounts are stated exclusive of value added taxes (VAT), which will be added to invoices where applicable.

6.3 The Consultant shall issue invoices for the Charges in accordance with the invoicing schedule set out in Schedule 1.

6.4 The Client must pay the Charges within **14 days** following the issue of an invoice.

6.5 If the Client does not pay any amount properly due under this Agreement, the Consultant may charge interest on the overdue amount at the rate of 8% per annum above the Bank of England base rate from time to time (which interest will accrue daily until the date of actual payment), or claim interest and statutory compensation pursuant to the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.

---

**7. Warranties**

7.1 The Consultant warrants that:
- she has the legal right and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations;
- she will comply with all applicable legal and regulatory requirements; and
- she has the necessary know-how, expertise and experience to perform the Services.

7.2 The Client warrants that it has the legal right and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations.

---

**8. Limitations of Liability**

8.1 Nothing in this Agreement will limit or exclude liability for death or personal injury resulting from negligence, or for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation.

8.2 Neither party shall be liable to the other in respect of any loss of profits, anticipated savings, revenue, or indirect or consequential loss or damage.

8.3 The Consultant's total aggregate liability to the Client under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the total Charges paid by the Client in the three months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

---

**9. Termination**

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving to the other party at least **14 days'** written notice of termination.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by giving written notice if the other party commits a material breach of this Agreement that is not remedied within 7 days of written notice of that breach.

9.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other party becomes insolvent, enters administration, or ceases to carry on business.

9.4 Upon termination, the Client shall pay all Charges due for Services performed up to the date of termination.

---

**10. Status of Consultant**

10.1 The Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee, worker, or agent of the Client. Nothing in this Agreement shall create or imply any employment relationship.

---

**11. General**

11.1 No breach of any provision of this Agreement shall be waived except with the express written consent of the party not in breach.

11.2 If any provision of this Agreement is found to be unlawful or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.

11.3 This Agreement may not be varied except by a written document signed by or on behalf of each of the parties.

11.4 Neither party may assign, transfer, or otherwise deal with any contractual rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

11.5 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to the subject matter and supersedes all previous agreements, arrangements and understandings between the parties.

11.6 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English and Welsh law.

11.7 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to adjudicate any dispute arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
