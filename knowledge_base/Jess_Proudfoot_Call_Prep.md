## 3. Key Talking Points & The "Earned-First" Proposition

### The Proposition
Do not pitch this as "getting giffgaff mentioned by ChatGPT." Instead, use your signature framing:
> *"Earned-first strategy strengthens the trusted public information environment around a brand. We measure whether that evidence is authoritative, retrievable, and accurately reflected across media, search, and AI-assisted discovery — and whether it contributes to audience and business outcomes."*

### The "Cultural Impact V2.0" Angle
Splendid has used "Cultural Impact" as their measurement philosophy for ~5 years. Position your Modern Earned Media Measurement Framework as **Cultural Impact V2.0**. It's the same core belief — earned media drives real-world outcomes — but updated with a new layer of evidence: **Brand Visibility Across Search & AI**. This makes the transition feel like an evolution, not a rip-and-replace.

### The 20–30s Audience Connection
giffgaff's critical priority is growing an engaged 20–30s audience (the "aspirational compromisers"). Connect this directly to AI search strategy:
- This demographic is disproportionately using AI-native search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, TikTok, Reddit Answers) rather than traditional Google.
- They search using "situations" (e.g., *"I'm travelling around Europe for 3 months"*) rather than keywords (*"roaming deals"*).
- Therefore, winning AI visibility for this audience requires community-sourced, problem-framed content — which plays directly to Splendid's earned-media strengths.
