Hey friends,
I can finally say the thing… I am… an author?!
Rand Fishkin and I are so excited to announce the thing we have obsessed over for 15 months:
Zero Click Marketing: How to Build Brands and Earn Customers on a Traffic-Starved Web
.
Pre-orders opened today. It comes out this fall.
I first coined the term “zero-click content” back in 2022. I had no idea it would become a thing, let alone define the next era of marketing.
The short version of why this book exists:
Brands and their websites are starving for traffic. But most marketing teams are still marketing like it’s 2018.
Google answers the question on-page for two-thirds of searches. Small publishers lost about 60% of their Google traffic last year. Social platforms actively punish you for linking out. ChatGPT summarizes your blog post before anyone ever opens it. AI referrals? Less than 1% of open-web traffic, despite every headline telling you otherwise.
And your boss still wants a last-click attribution report by Friday 🙄
Zero Click Marketing
is the honest, tactical, occasionally unhinged, and aggressively footnoted guide to earning attention, building an audience, and growing a business on a web that has mostly stopped sending clicks.
It’s not a “how to go viral on TikTok” manual. (Good luck out there!) It’s also not one of those More-Successful-Than-Thou airport business books where every chapter should have been a blog post.
It’s the playbook Rand and I have been running ourselves for years. It works. We have the receipts.
