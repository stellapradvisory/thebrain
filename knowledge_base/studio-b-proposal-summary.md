# Studio B × giffgaff Proposal: Strategic Summary
*Compiled July 2026 for Splendid Communications*

This document summarises the 52-page Studio B proposal pitched to giffgaff. While this is a separate initiative to Splendid’s immediate brief, it provides critical intelligence on how giffgaff is currently thinking about member engagement, content platforms, and AI search discoverability.

---

## 1. Core Proposition: "The Board"

Studio B has proposed a multi-format, member-engagement platform rather than a conventional advertising campaign. The hero concept is **"The Board"** — a six-part YouTube series that gamifies business and financial education for young adults.

The premise targets "aspirational compromisers" (20–30-year-olds navigating side hustles and non-traditional career paths). The show features a real early-stage entrepreneur (the "Player") navigating a physical game board of business dilemmas, judged by two contrasting experts (the "Masterminds" — one legacy builder, one digital-first creator). 

The strategic goal is to position giffgaff as a trusted, practical ally that equips members with real-world knowledge, aligning with giffgaff's ambition to be seen as a utility brand like Monzo or Google.

## 2. Content Architecture & Distribution

The proposal outlines a highly structured "1 into many" content engine designed for behavioural platform fit:

| Content Layer | Format | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero** | 20–30 min YouTube episode | The definitive, premium master edit. |
| **Hub** | 9–15 vertical videos | Short-form debate and dilemmas for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. |
| **Hygiene** | 3–5 statics/carousels | "Cheat codes" and life-hack infographics. |
| **Owned** | App exclusives | Member-only insights and behind-the-scenes content to drive app usage. |

Crucially, Studio B recommends launching this on **giffgaff’s existing YouTube channel** to leverage existing subscriber base and SEO equity, rather than starting a new channel from scratch.

## 3. AI-Native Design & GEO Integration

Perhaps the most significant intelligence for Splendid is that **Studio B has built AI search discoverability (GEO) directly into the format.** 

The proposal explicitly argues that search has shifted from link-scanning to AI-generated answers, and that "The Board" is designed to generate the structured, expert-led content that Large Language Models (LLMs) cite. 

Their five AI-native design principles are:
1. **Full verbatim transcripts** published as structured blog articles.
2. An evergreen **"Cheat Code" knowledge base** targeting specific queries.
3. **Expert attribution markup** so masterminds become citeable sources.
4. **Canonical YouTube metadata** (chapters, timestamps) treated as editorial copy.
5. A **cross-platform citation network** linking blog, YouTube, and community.

They also propose a measurement framework for LLM visibility, tracking AI Overview appearances and citation counts in ChatGPT and Perplexity. **This validates Splendid's own GEO approach and confirms giffgaff is already receptive to AI-native content strategies.**

## 4. Commercials & Delivery

Studio B pitches themselves as an end-to-end, in-house production facility (35 staff, 10,000 sq ft studio), removing third-party friction and cost. 

- **Budget:** The Phase 0 proof-of-concept (6 episodes plus full social ecosystem) is priced at a flat **£150,000** (net of VAT, including talent and insurance, excluding paid media).
- **Timeline:** They commit to an 8-week turnaround from greenlight to Episode 1 going live, explicitly building in giffgaff's three-round approval process.
- **Monetisation:** The long-term roadmap (Phases 1–4) envisions the format becoming owned IP that giffgaff can monetise through live events, podcast sponsorships, and format licensing.

## 5. Strategic Implications for Splendid

1. **The Bar is High:** giffgaff is entertaining sophisticated, multi-platform, AI-integrated content models. Any proactive pitch from Splendid must match this level of operational and strategic depth.
2. **AI Search is a Shared Priority:** The fact that Studio B includes a dedicated section on LLM discoverability means Hellen Benavides (AI Search Lead) is likely a key stakeholder in these content decisions. Splendid’s GEO intelligence is highly relevant to this conversation.
3. **Focus on "Fairness" and "Utility":** The Studio B pitch leans heavily into giffgaff's "Play Fair" standard, providing practical utility rather than vague inspiration. Splendid’s ideas should similarly focus on equipping members with useful tools or knowledge.
