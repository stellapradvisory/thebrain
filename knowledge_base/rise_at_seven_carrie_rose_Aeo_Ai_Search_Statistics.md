AEO/AI Search & Citation Statistics 2026
AEO Search
Share
AI search is now where answers happen.
ChatGPT has reached 900 million weekly active users, Google's AI Overviews appear on more than 20% of searches, and 68% of US Google searches now end without a single click to a website.
Answer Engine Optimisation
(AEO) - earning citations inside AI-generated answers, has become a visibility requirement, not an experiment.
Yet only 20% of marketing teams have started implementing it.
Here are the latest AEO statistics on AI search usage, zero-click behaviour, citation patterns, referral traffic, and what actually increases AI visibility.
AEO Statistics 2026 (Top Picks)
ChatGPT has 900 million weekly active users, up from 400 million a year earlier.
Google AI Mode passed 1 billion monthly users, with query volume more than doubling every quarter.
AI Overviews now appear on 20%+ of Google searches — and click-through rates drop by nearly 60% when they do.
68% of US Google searches end without a click to any website.
Only 1% of users click a source link inside a Google AI summary.
ChatGPT referral traffic to websites grew 206% year-over-year.
Content optimization techniques can boost AI citation visibility by up to 40% (Princeton GEO study).
70% of marketers believe AEO will significantly impact their strategy within 1–3 years - but only 20% have begun implementing it.
How Many People Use AI Search?
1) ChatGPT Has 900 Million Weekly Active Users
OpenAI reported that ChatGPT passed 900 million weekly active users in early 2026 - more than double the 400 million reported in February 2025. By June 2026, Reuters reported the app had reached 1 billion monthly active users, the fastest app in history to reach that milestone.
ChatGPT now processes roughly 2.5 billion prompts per day.

2) Google AI Mode Has Passed 1 Billion Monthly Users
Google announced at I/O 2026 that AI Mode, its fully generative search interface, surpassed 1 billion monthly users, with query volume more than doubling each quarter.
For context: clickstream data from January–April 2026 showed AI Mode at just 0.34% of all Google searches during that window, meaning the behavioural impact of AI Mode has barely begun.

3) AI Search Visits Grew 42.8% Year-Over-Year — Google Grew Just 2.4%
Between Q1 2025 and Q1 2026, visits to AI search platforms climbed from 15.6 billion to 27.4 billion - a 42.8% increase. Over the same window, Google search visits grew 2.4%.
The absolute gap is still enormous, but the growth-rate gap is the story: attention is compounding toward answer engines.
Source:
Similarweb data via

4) ChatGPT's Share Of Generative AI Traffic Has Fallen From 76% To ~53%
AEO is no longer a one-platform game. Similarweb's 2026 Generative AI Landscape data shows ChatGPT's share of worldwide generative AI web traffic declining from roughly 76% a year ago to around 53%, while Gemini has climbed past a quarter of all traffic and Claude has become the fastest-growing platform in the category.
Optimising for a single AI platform used to cover most of the addressable audience. It no longer does.
