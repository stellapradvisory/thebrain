## The "Play Fair" Standard

From the Studio B proposal context and giffgaff's news site, giffgaff's content standard is built around one question: **"Does this make life a bit fairer for our members?"** This is the editorial filter for all content — blog, social, YouTube, community.

The four content pillars of the "Play On" platform:
1. **Beat the Game** — calling out unfair systems (loyalty penalties, hidden contracts) → Blog/Editorial Hub
2. **Smart Moves** — practical intelligence, life hacks, community-sourced tips → Social (primary, 60%)
3. **Back Yourself** — helping members start, fund or build something → Events (Jul 2026)
4. **Game Changers** — honest accounts of people who saw an unfair system and did something about it → Community (2026)
