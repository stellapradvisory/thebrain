5 things to take away

PR's Data Structure Problem — AMEC Global Summit 2026

01 PR has a data structure problem, not just a 
measurement problem
The root cause is fragmented classiﬁcation and 
inconsistent tagging — not the tools, the 
dashboards, or the frameworks.

04 Fixed drivers, ﬂexible outcomes

Four ﬁxed drivers — Activity Type, Published Format, 
Sentiment, and Brand Prominence — create a stable 
classiﬁcation layer beneath whatever objectives and 
outcomes change by campaign.

02 Reporting and insight are not the same thing

Reporting tells you what happened. Structured data 
tells you what's happening — and what to do next, 
while there's still time to act.

03 AI is exposing the weakness, not ﬁxing it
Inconsistent inputs produce unreliable AI outputs. 
You cannot automate your way out of a 
classiﬁcation problem.

05

Shared structure is the industry's biggest 
unlocked opportunity
Without it, every campaign is an island. With it, PR 
can benchmark credibly, learn collectively, and 
demonstrate value with the same conﬁdence as paid 
media or SEO.
